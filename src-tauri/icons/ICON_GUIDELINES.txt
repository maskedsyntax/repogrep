macOS app icons (Big Sur / Apple HIG) — repogrep

1. Source of truth
   - Master file: source-app-icon.png (1024×1024, PNG).
   - Update: copy your final artwork over source-app-icon.png, then regenerate:
       npm run icons:generate
     (equivalent: npx tauri icon src-tauri/icons/source-app-icon.png)

2. What you provide (design rules — not automated here)
   - Full-bleed square canvas; do not bake the squircle or rounded “app icon” shape into the PNG.
   - Prefer ~10–15% safe margin in the artwork so nothing important sits on the outer edge (HIG).
   - Avoid stacking your own heavy drop shadow on top of what the system will add (can look muddy).

3. What the system does
   - The installed .app uses icon.icns; the OS composes the dock / Finder tile (mask, lighting, shadow).

4. tauri dev vs packaged app
   - For the real dock/Finder look, build and open the .app:
       npm run tauri build
       open src-tauri/target/release/bundle/macos/*.app
